      Model and coverage

      Calls contribute positive gamma; puts negative. The call and put walls are the largest call-gamma and absolute put-gamma concentrations separately. This sign convention is a positioning proxy: open interest does not identify dealer holdings. Both signed net and gross exposure are calculated.

      Gamma uses published mark IV, each expiry's forward/index ratio and ACT/365 time. GEX = signed index-delta gamma × open interest in base coins × index price² × 1%. Deribit API options open interest already includes contract sizing; it is never multiplied by contract size again. Missing forwards or incomplete summaries exclude that settlement market. Listed option settlements and native strike currencies are discovered from Deribit instrument metadata. Strikes and forwards are converted to the selected quote using observed currency references. Exposure is measured in {{ cfg.symbol.split('/')[-1] }} delta per 1% index move.

      The flip reprices the chain with fixed IV, open interest and forward/index ratios over ±30% of the index. The nearest detected sign change is shown; there may be none or several.
