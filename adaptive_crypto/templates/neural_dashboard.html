Model and execution settings

Parente 5/2: 36 inputs → 128 → 64 → 32 → BUY / HOLD / SELL. This is the authors' published model, calibrated through {{ time(nn_model.trained_through_ms) if nn_model.get('trained_through_ms') else 'unavailable' }}.

Automatic paper entries require a qualified Perky SMC setup and current Parente BUY confirmation. The plan supplies its structure stop and TP1–TP3. A stop hit, TP3 hit or later Parente SELL closes the remaining position. TP1 and TP2 are intermediate levels; there is no partial target exit.

Parente uses Binance spot/USDT four-hour candles, frozen feature scaler and base-volume calibration. SMC, charts and paper prices use the selected dashboard pair. Deribit supplies supported markets; Binance spot supplies pairs absent from Deribit, or all pairs when selected in Settings. Parente confirmations and exits retain the original Binance/USDT inputs. Paper fills apply configured fees and slippage to the selected market price; composite indices are reference prices.
