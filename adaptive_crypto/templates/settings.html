Analysis and simulation settings

A qualified Perky SMC setup supplies the paper entry, configured stop and TP1–TP3. Parente BUY must confirm the entry. The recorded stop, TP3 or a later Parente SELL closes the paper position; TP1 and TP2 are intermediate levels. GEX can guide targets for existing positions.

Paper equity, floor and minimum notional are USD. Rates and risk values are fractions: 0.005 = 0.5%. Fields ending in bps use basis points. Perky signals and the chart use the selected 1M, 5M, 15M, 30M or 4H timeframe.

Parente 5/2

Published trained model · 36 inputs → 128 → 64 → 32 → BUY / HOLD / SELL. Published weights, original feature scaler and frozen Binance base-volume calibration.

NN inputs: Binance spot/USDT · completed 4H candles · base-asset volume. SMC, charts, position prices and targets use your selected market pair; this never changes the trained NN inputs.
